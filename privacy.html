Privacy Policy

What information this site collects, and how it's used.

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

The only personal information collected through this site is what you submit voluntarily through the contact form on the Contact page: your name, organisation, email address, sector, subject, and message. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not use cookies beyond what's strictly required for the site to function.

How the Contact Form Works

The contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards your submission to our email address. Formspree's own privacy practices govern the brief handling of your submission in transit; we do not share your submission with any other third party.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry.
  • To understand which service area you're enquiring about, so we can respond appropriately.

We don't use your information for marketing, and we don't sell or share it with anyone.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to it and maintain a record of the engagement, and delete it on request.

Your Rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it. Contact tony@port254.com for any of these requests.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the update date above will change accordingly.
