Who We Are

Port254 is the principal-led advisory practice of Tony Kirui — more than two decades across networking, infrastructure and cybersecurity, currently operating at Principal Advisor/Consultant level with critical infrastructure and operational-technology environments in Australia. Port254 applies that experience directly to Africa's critical infrastructure: energy, water, transport and financial services operators navigating both the operational-technology risk and the regulatory obligations now attached to it.
